📌 Call logic (fixed)
  • Count consecutive INACTIVE days per customer.
  • When idle days reach the bucket Idle Threshold, a call is queued (threshold met that day).
  • BOT places the call on a later day (typically next day), but only if the bucket Cooldown since the last BOT call has elapsed.
  • If cooldown is not ready yet, the queued call stays pending and is placed on the first eligible day.
  • If the customer becomes ACTIVE, the pending call is cancelled and the idle streak resets.
  • After a successful call, idle streak resets; another full threshold is required for the next call (still subject to cooldown).
